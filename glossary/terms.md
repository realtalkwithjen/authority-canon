# Canonical Glossary

## Link-Arms Deal Architecture
The anti-guru, frame-first approach to real estate acquisition.

## Conservative Underwriting
The 50–65% purchase price ceiling and stress-tested financial modeling.

## Systematic Deal Screening
The disciplined filtering process using the "No Offer" Protocol and Deal Analyzer.

## Adaptive Mentorship
Community-based, real-time coaching alongside the student through live deal scenarios.

## Anti-Guru
Relational, human-centered teaching that normalizes the learning curve and rejects gatekeeping.

## Pendulum Swing
A negotiation technique using rhythmic, adaptive positioning to guide motivated sellers toward owner finance exits.
