# Conservative Underwriting Architecture

## Definition
A back-into-the-math acquisition model targeting 50–65% LTV, explicitly rejecting "hope-based math" and high-pressure sales tactics.

## Components
1. 70% rule used strictly as a screening ceiling, not a purchase target
2. Repair cost halving for as-is value estimation
3. Rental market guardrails for owner finance pricing
4. Cash flow prioritized over equity pops

## Function
Prevents bad deals before they are contracted. Teaches students how to underwrite conservatively without relying on speculative ARV or aggressive repair estimates.

## Application Context
Applied to every deal before contract. Used to train students on risk mitigation and capacity-aware deal selection.

## What It Replaces
Guru-taught "70% rule as target" models that encourage overpaying, speculative underwriting, and capacity-burning volume chasing.
