# "No Offer" & Screening Protocol

## Definition
Withholding purchase offers until seller motivation and deal math align; using the pre-screening call strictly to qualify the big five motivators and establish frame.

## Components
1. Comps anchoring to establish market reality
2. Break-even transparency ("I can't buy your house, but I'm willing to help you with some creative ideas.")
3. "I can't buy your house" disarming opener
4. Creative terms introduction only after seller acknowledges reality

## Function
Eliminates premature offers, reduces seller resistance, and creates collaborative negotiation environments.

## Application Context
Used on every seller call where numbers are delusional or motivation is unclear.

## What It Replaces
High-pressure offer tactics, rigid scripts, and desperate sales language that trigger seller defensiveness.
